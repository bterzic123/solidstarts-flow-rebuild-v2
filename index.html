What this is

We went through Solid Starts the way a new parent does, then rebuilt the parts we think leave money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what actually moves conversion and revenue across the market, not inside one app. We pointed that at Solid Starts: installed it, answered every question about a six-month-old called Lana, read your App Store listing and your pricing page, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: your speech bubbles, the blue help cards, the date wheel, the cream selected state, your loader ring and your plan cards. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
