# Growth Audit — Solid Starts

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/solidstarts-flow-rebuild-v2/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Solid Starts already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Solid Starts' own design. Only the order of the screens changes; the art direction does not.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Where it lands | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Catch the parents who close the paywall: your monthly plan, without a trial, next to what the free version leaves out | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask for an account after the paywall, not on the first screen | Welcome, Account | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Put the baby's name and the help area they picked in the paywall headline | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Show your 4.9 stars, 6M users, App of the Day and a matching parent quote where people pay | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Let the loader read their answers back, and carry them onto the journey screen | Loader, Journey | CR +10–15% |

Run together: up to **+25% conversion to purchase** and **+30% ARPU**.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Solid Starts' own App Store listing, website, pricing page and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Solid Starts.
